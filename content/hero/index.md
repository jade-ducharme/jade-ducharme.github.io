I'm a Ph.D. candidate in physics at Brown University, working on radio interferometry and 21-cm cosmology.

My research focuses on detecting and mitigating radio frequency interference. These (usually) man-made signals corrupt astronomical data, preventing a clean detection of the 21-cm neutral hydrogen line.
