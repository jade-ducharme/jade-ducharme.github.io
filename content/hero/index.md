I'm a Ph.D. candidate in physics at Brown University, working on radio interferometry and 21-cm cosmology with the [Murchison Widefield Array](https://www.mwatelescope.org/).

My research focuses on detecting and mitigating radio frequency interference — the human-made signals that stand between our telescopes and the first billion years of cosmic history.
