Hello! I'm Jade, a Ph.D. candidate in physics at [Brown University](https://www.brown.edu/academics/physics/), where I work with [Prof. Jonathan Pober](https://sites.brown.edu/jpober/) on radio interferometry and 21-cm cosmology.

I study the Epoch of Reionization — the era when the first stars and galaxies lit up the universe. The faint 21-cm hydrogen signal from that period is buried under terrestrial radio noise many orders of magnitude brighter, so much of my work is about **finding and removing radio frequency interference** before it corrupts a cosmological measurement.

Recently I've been building Bayesian frameworks for flagging RFI, modeling moving interference sources like satellites, and using interferometric near-field corrections to estimate how high above the array those sources actually are.

I earned my M.Sc. at Brown in 2025 and my B.Sc. in physics at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/physics/) in 2022. I've been a member of the [MWA Collaboration](https://www.mwatelescope.org/) since 2023, and outside research I coordinate and build the site for [Astronomy on Tap Rhode Island](https://aot-ri.vercel.app). I work in French (native) and English (fluent).
